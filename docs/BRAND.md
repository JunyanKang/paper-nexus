# Paper Nexus

The mark is a compact three-node connection. Teal, ivory and restrained champagne preserve the visual relationship with Paper Voice, while the new silhouette emphasizes linked research rather than a book or mascot.

The transparent production PNG is `addon/assets/nexus.png`, resized to 256 px for distribution. The Reader uses a 24 px icon-only button with a Paper Nexus tooltip and accessible name. Use the same asset in the add-on manager, literature panel, network header and settings. Hover cards remain free of repeated decoration.

## Production prompt

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-05:

```text
Create a single finished premium application icon for Paper Nexus, a serious scientific literature reading and local research connections tool. This is a complete replacement of a book mascot: make the connection motif dominant and do not depict a book, pages, headphones, face, eyes, hands, letters or text. Icon concept: a compact sculptural nexus of exactly three substantial rounded pebble-like nodes connected by two broad, gently curved satin-teal bridges; the overall connected silhouette quietly suggests a flowing diagonal N without literally drawing a letter. One deep petrol-teal node at lower left, a warm ivory node near center-right, a small restrained champagne-gold node at upper left, with a subtle dark teal outline on the ivory piece so it reads on a white background. Three nodes only; no molecule/atom, orbital rings, tangled graph, stars, sparks, extra dots or decorative background. Make it elegant and calm, a little organic and tactile like finely crafted matte ceramic and anodized metal, with minimal soft dimensional shading, not a glossy toy. Strong simple silhouette with robust connector thickness and large negative spaces so it remains recognizable at 20, 24 and 32 pixels. Balanced centered square composition, icon occupies 86 percent of canvas; no tiny texture, no fragile thin line, no cast shadow outside silhouette, no tile or enclosing square. Main colors deep teal #225E58, warm ivory #F2E9D4, small champagne #C5A267 accent. True transparent background with clean alpha edges. Produce one single icon only, no presentation board, no mockup, no alternatives, no labels, no text.
```

Validate at 20, 24, 32, 48 and 64 CSS pixels on light, paper and dark surfaces. Installed icons must load from the package, never a development path. Historical release records retain their original names and artwork.
